# Insurance Group — Confident, clear, considered

An international travel insurance demo. English (default), Russian, German. The customer should understand total cost, coverage, important exclusions, and the next useful action.

## Reference and own identity

The user selected Revolut as a design and UX quality benchmark. The public [pricing comparison](https://www.revolut.com/our-pricing-plans/) informed confident typography, restrained navigation, rounded actions and clear product hierarchy. Insurance Group retains its warm canvas, cobalt, Source Sans 3 and travel-focused compositions.

The user rejected the earlier template-like composition. Avoid repeated slogans, decorative eyebrow labels, floating mock cards and equal-weight boxes around every piece of information. Establish a useful hierarchy through alignment, scale, spacing and real product content. Each screen should expose its main action and relevant records without a stack of introductory sections.
